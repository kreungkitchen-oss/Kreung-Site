What is kroeung?

The paste that makes it Khmer

Kroeung is the herb paste at the heart of Cambodian cooking: lemongrass, galangal, turmeric, kaffir lime and garlic, pounded together. We marinate everything in it, then grill it hot over open flame.

Kreung LA is run by Sam and Christina, cooking the Cambodian BBQ we grew up on and sharing it with Los Angeles one event at a time.

    FAQ

    How far ahead should I book?

    Two weeks is ideal, and a month or more for weddings and events over 150 guests. Last-minute? Ask anyway.

    What areas do you serve?

    All of Los Angeles County. We travel to Orange County and the Inland Empire for a small travel fee.

    Do you handle dietary restrictions?

    Yes. We can do vegetarian and gluten-free options. Tell us about allergies in the quote form.

    Is there a minimum order?

    Our minimum is 20 guests. Live grill service starts at 50 guests.

    How do deposits and payment work?

    A 50% deposit holds your date, with the balance due a week before the event. We send an invoice you can pay online.
